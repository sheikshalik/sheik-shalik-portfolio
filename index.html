About me

Science-led, technology-enabled agriculture with a focus on practical outcomes.

My academic work focuses on nutrient and weed management in rice-based systems, with wider interests in climate-smart agriculture, agricultural waste valorisation, UAV-based monitoring, remote sensing, GIS and data-driven farming. I also enjoy teaching, presenting and sharing technical knowledge with students and professionals.
