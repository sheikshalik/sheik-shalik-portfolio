A little about my work

Field-based agronomy, connected to the tools shaping agriculture’s next chapter.

Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences · 2017–2021 · OGPA 8.01 (80%)

My academic work focuses on nutrient and weed management in rice-based systems, with wider interests in climate-smart agriculture, agricultural waste valorisation and sustainable crop production.

I am interested in how UAV-based monitoring, remote sensing, GIS and data analysis can support field research and practical agricultural decision-making. My technical training includes a DGCA Remote Pilot Certificate for small-category rotorcraft.

Alongside research, I contribute through scientific writing, conference presentations, peer review, teaching and knowledge sharing with students and professionals.
